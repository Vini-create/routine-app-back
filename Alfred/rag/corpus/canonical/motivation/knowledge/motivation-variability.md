# Variability in Motivational State

## Operational definition

A motivational state is a time-varying willingness to initiate or sustain goal-directed effort. It is distinct from whether the goal is endorsed and from a stable description of the person.

## What this concept does not mean

Motivation is not irrelevant, and low motivation does not always call for acting anyway. A sharp or persistent change can reflect sleep, health, mood, overload, context or loss of goal endorsement.

## Main mechanisms

Momentary willingness changes the subjective balance between effort and reward. A prior cue-linked plan can reduce the need to make the entire decision again at the action point, but only after a goal is chosen.

## Evidence summary

An intensive longitudinal study sampled adults repeatedly over 15 days and found substantial state variation; momentary motivation prospectively predicted reward sensitivity and effort-based choices. The design does not show that state motivation caused every subsequent choice or that plans eliminate state effects. A meta-analysis of implementation intentions found average benefits for goal achievement while retaining dependence on prior intention, context and plan quality.

## Evidence mapping

- Motivation varied within people and related prospectively to effort-based choice: `src-motivation-fluctuation-2025` (`intensive_longitudinal_study`).
- If-then planning supported goal striving on average: `src-ii-2006` (`meta_analysis`).

## Relevant signals

The user postpones until a preferred feeling arrives, action varies with daily willingness despite recurring opportunities, or a brief surge drives an oversized plan.

## Alternative explanations

The goal may be imposed, the task aversive, capacity insufficient, or the opportunity poorly specified.

## Information needed

Whether the goal remains chosen, recent motivation and action pattern, sleep or capacity changes, actual opportunity, minimum useful action and safety signals.

## Practical implications

Treat motivation as information and a variable condition. If the goal remains endorsed and capacity is adequate, plan a feasible action anchored to an opportunity and review what happened across several occasions.

## Limitations

The longitudinal study measured effort-based decisions, not every routine behavior. This concept is not an assessment of depression, apathy or another health condition.
