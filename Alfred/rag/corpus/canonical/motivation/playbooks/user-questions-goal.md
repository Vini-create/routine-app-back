# User Questions the Value or Ownership of a Goal

## Activation criteria

The user explicitly questions the value, ownership or continued desirability of
a goal.

## Similar situations that should not activate this playbook

Do not activate when the demonstrated barrier is insufficient time, missing
skill, unavailable resources or tiredness while the goal remains endorsed.

## Possible explanations

The goal may be imposed, may have lost value, may conflict with a higher
priority, or may still matter while its current cost or plan is unacceptable.

## Missing information

Who set the goal, the real consequences of refusing it, any personally endorsed
value, the available degree of choice and whether the user wants analysis,
options or simply acknowledgment.

## Response objective

Clarify the decision without manufacturing motivation or treating abandonment
as failure.

## Decision path

- If the goal is rejected and optional, consider ending or replacing it.
- If a real obligation exists, separate the required minimum from an idealized version.
- If the outcome remains valued but current cost is excessive, review scope, plan or deadline.
- If the user wants acknowledgment rather than options, do not continue into planning.

## Candidate strategies

Goal discontinuation, goal substitution, minimum-obligation definition, value
clarification and goal review.

## Conditions for selecting each strategy

Use discontinuation or substitution when choice is genuine, minimum-obligation
definition when consequences are fixed, and goal review when value remains but
the current arrangement is unsustainable.

## When to ask a question

Ask when the presence of a real obligation or personally endorsed value changes
the branch and is not yet known.

## When to respond directly

Respond directly when the user clearly rejects an optional goal or clearly
identifies an obligation and the desired form of help.

## What to avoid

Do not persuade, use theory to invalidate the decision, or assume every loss of
execution is a motivation problem.

## Suggested next step

Record the explicit choice: end, replace, define the required minimum or review
the plan for a still-valued outcome.
