# Functional Pattern of Procrastination

## Operational definition

Procrastination is voluntary delay despite expecting the delay to make the
outcome worse. A functional pattern describes the antecedent, alternative
action, immediate payoff and later cost before selecting an explanation.

## What this concept does not mean

Not every delay is procrastination. Missing time, information, access, ability,
decision authority or safety can make postponement reasonable or unavoidable.
The concept is not a personality label or clinical diagnosis.

## Main mechanisms

Task aversiveness, delayed rewards, low expectancy and access to more immediate
rewards can shift behavior away from an intended task. These are candidate
mechanisms; an episode-level description is needed to distinguish them from
practical constraints.

## Evidence summary

Steel's meta-analysis reported associations between procrastination and factors
including task aversiveness, reward delay, self-efficacy and impulsiveness. A
systematic review of psychological treatments found a small, heterogeneous
overall benefit across few trials. Neither synthesis identifies one universal
cause or intervention.

## Evidence mapping

- Task aversiveness, delay, self-efficacy and impulsiveness showed relevant aggregate associations: `src-procrastination-steel-2007` (`meta_analysis`).
- Psychological treatments showed a small heterogeneous benefit in a limited trial base: `src-procrastination-treatment-2018` (`systematic_review`).

## Relevant signals

The person performs peripheral preparation, switches to a more immediately
rewarding activity or waits for deadline pressure while still intending to do
the task.

## Alternative explanations

Strategic delay, a competing priority, incomplete instructions, exhaustion,
pain, an imposed goal or dependency on another person can produce similar
outward delay.

## Information needed

The chosen task, deadline, a recent episode, the alternative action, its
immediate consequence, expected later cost, available skill and resources.

## Practical implications

Match any strategy to the best-supported episode mechanism and identify an
observation that would contradict that explanation. Avoid applying a generic
anti-procrastination list.

## Limitations

Correlates are not individual causes. Persistent or disabling distress is
outside the scope of a routine-coaching explanation.
