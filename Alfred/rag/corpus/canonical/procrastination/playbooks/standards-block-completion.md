# Standards Block Completion or Submission

## Activation criteria

Revision, delay or non-submission is linked to a quality standard or anticipated
evaluation, and at least one version already exists or could be produced.

## Similar situations that should not activate this playbook

Do not activate when the work objectively misses an explicit requirement, the
user lacks a prerequisite skill, or the user has decided not to submit.

## Possible explanations

The completion criterion may be undefined, the cost of a minor error may be
overestimated, exposure to evaluation may be avoided, or an external standard
may be genuinely strict.

## Missing information

Explicit requirements, actual consequences of error, who evaluates the work,
the versions already produced, the remaining skill gap and the deadline.

## Response objective

Distinguish required quality from revision that no longer changes the relevant
outcome, then define a verifiable completion or learning decision.

## Decision path

- If requirements are explicit and unmet, convert them into a finite checklist.
- If requirements are met but completion is undefined, set a completion criterion and revision boundary.
- If a specific skill is missing, practice that component before requiring submission.
- If the user does not intend to submit, review the goal rather than forcing completion.

## Candidate strategies

Requirement checklist, completion criterion, revision limit, graded exposure to
feedback and focused skill practice.

## Conditions for selecting each strategy

Use a checklist for external criteria, a revision limit when later passes produce
no relevant change, graded exposure when evaluation is the observed barrier and
focused practice when errors identify a concrete skill gap.

## When to ask a question

Ask when the distinction between an unmet requirement and an undefined standard
is not available from the user's description.

## When to respond directly

Respond directly when the stated requirements and repeated revision cycles make
the applicable branch clear.

## What to avoid

Do not recommend accepting any quality level, diagnose perfectionism, or lower a
real professional, academic or safety requirement.

## Suggested next step

Choose the next verifiable boundary: complete one unmet requirement, stop after
a defined review pass, or request targeted feedback.
