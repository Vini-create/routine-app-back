# User Reports Insufficient Capacity

## Activation criteria

Known commitments and realistic durations exceed available windows, or two
priorities compete for the same limited resource.

## Similar situations that should not activate this playbook

Do not activate from postponement alone. Ambiguity, missing skill, non-endorsed
goals and tiredness require different decisions.

## Possible explanations

Demand may genuinely exceed capacity; duration may be underestimated; priority
may be implicit; or an obligation may require renegotiation with another person.

## Missing information

Fixed commitments, realistic durations, deadlines, consequences of delay and
which commitments can be removed, delegated, reduced or renegotiated.

## Response objective

Make the capacity conflict explicit and select a tradeoff instead of adding
productivity techniques to an impossible schedule.

## Decision path

- If total demand exceeds capacity, identify what must be removed, delegated, delayed or renegotiated.
- If two priorities conflict, compare their current consequences and select one explicitly.
- If duration is uncertain, observe one representative execution before rebuilding the schedule.
- If the goal has lost priority, review or pause it rather than repeatedly shrinking the task.

## Candidate strategies

Demand removal, delegation, deadline negotiation, priority selection, duration
sampling and goal review.

## Conditions for selecting each strategy

Use removal or negotiation for arithmetic overload, duration sampling when the
estimate drives the conflict, and goal review when the objective no longer
justifies its opportunity cost.

## When to ask a question

Ask when one missing duration, consequence or degree of control changes the
tradeoff.

## When to respond directly

Respond directly when incompatibility is already explicit and the remaining
decision is which commitment moves.

## What to avoid

Do not present optimization as creation of time, add an unprioritized task list
or assume that every obligation is negotiable.

## Suggested next step

Name one commitment to remove, move, delegate, renegotiate or protect, together
with the consequence of that choice.
