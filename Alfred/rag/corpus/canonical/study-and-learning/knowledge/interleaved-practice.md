# Interleaved Practice

## Operational definition

Interleaved practice mixes examples or problem types from different categories so the learner must discriminate which category, concept or procedure applies. Blocked practice groups one type together.

## What this concept does not mean

Interleaving is not random multitasking, rapid switching between unrelated subjects or a universal replacement for initial instruction and blocked familiarization.

## Main mechanisms

Adjacent contrast can highlight differences between categories, and mixed problems require selecting a method rather than repeating a recently demonstrated procedure. Interleaving may also introduce spacing, so the two mechanisms can be difficult to separate.

## Evidence summary

A multilevel meta-analysis of 59 studies found a moderate overall interleaving effect but strong moderation by material: benefits were larger for some visual categories and mathematics, ambiguous for expository texts and reversed for word materials. Dunlosky and colleagues likewise treated interleaved practice as promising but less broadly established than practice testing and distributed practice.

## Evidence mapping

- The average interleaving effect depended substantially on material and similarity structure: `src-interleaving-meta-2019` (`meta_analysis`).
- Interleaving had narrower evidence than retrieval and distributed practice in a broad learning review: `src-learning-dunlosky-2013` (`evidence_review`).

## Relevant signals

The assessment requires distinguishing similar categories or selecting among procedures, while blocked practice produces success only when the method is obvious from order.

## Alternative explanations

Errors may reflect missing foundational knowledge, insufficient retrieval, inadequate feedback or practice that is too difficult.

## Information needed

Learning objective, category similarity, current mastery, test format, feedback availability and whether learners must select a method.

## Practical implications

Use a small set of related categories, require an explicit choice, provide corrective feedback and compare delayed discrimination with blocked practice. Retain blocks for initial acquisition when needed.

## Limitations

Effects do not generalize across all materials, and laboratory schedules differ from complete courses. Subjective difficulty is not proof of learning.
