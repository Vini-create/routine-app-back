# Canonical deduplication report

Date: 2026-07-14  
Scope: all 45 active Alfred-only documents after final editorial review.

## Literal duplication

No repeated prose block of at least 100 normalized characters was found across
the 28 knowledge documents and 17 playbooks. Shared schema headings were
excluded from prose-block comparison.

## Full-document similarity

All 990 document pairs were compared after lowercasing, whitespace
normalization and removal of punctuation. No pair reached the conservative
normalized-text similarity ratio of 0.45.

## Structural and operational review

- Knowledge documents retain evidence, alternatives and limitations without
  reproducing playbook decision trees.
- Playbooks refer to concept IDs and contain situation-specific branches rather
  than fixed response scripts.
- No playbook prescribes a universal seven-day test, automatic task reduction
  or mandatory question.
- Goal difficulty versus graded progression, action planning versus
  implementation intentions, and lapse recovery versus compensation remain
  separate because they answer different decisions.

## Result

The 45 active documents, document IDs and unit IDs are unique. The 17 playbooks
range from 298 to 430 words under the conservative diagnostic word-count proxy;
the canonical validator accepts all of them within the configured range.
